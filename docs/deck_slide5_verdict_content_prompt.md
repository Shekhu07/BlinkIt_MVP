# Design Labs prompt — slide 5: replace the oversized verdict band with triangulation content

Target slide: **slide 5**, "Confirmed the cause. Challenged the fix." (Primary Research)

This follows on from `deck_slide5_redesign_prompt.md` (header/count fix + slimming the
verdict band). That prompt shrinks the yellow verdict band; this one specifies what to
put in the space it frees up. Do the redesign prompt first if you haven't already — this
one assumes the band is already down to a compact single row.

## The two additions, and why they're safe to add
1. **A triangulation strip** — survey and interviews independently landing on the same
   finding. Uses only numbers already established elsewhere: the survey's "8 of 18 stuck
   respondents had no incident at all" (already on this slide, slide 5's "What the survey
   challenged" panel) and the interview tag "Low-intent, satisfied (1)" (already in the
   "Interviews & quotes" panel). No new data — just placed side by side.
2. **A 4th interview tag, "Assortment gap (1)"** — added to the existing 3-chip row
   (`Trust-driven, incident (1)` · `Trust-driven, risk (2)` · `Low-intent, satisfied (1)`).
   This is real: the 5th interview respondent cited lack of variety/assortment (electronics
   "got more options elsewhere," personal care "organic products" unavailable) as their
   reason for not trying a category — a cause distinct from trust, not previously
   represented in this slide's tags. With this chip, the 4 tags sum to 5, matching the
   corrected interview count.

## What NOT to touch
- Don't change any N=31 survey stat, donut percentage, or the "Asked directly" bar.
- Don't change the 3 existing quote cards or their attributions.
- Don't touch quantitative validation panel or interviews/quotes panel width/position.
- Don't invent any number not listed above — if it's not in this prompt or already on
  the slide, it doesn't go on the slide.
