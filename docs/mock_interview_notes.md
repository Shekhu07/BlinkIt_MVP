## Candidate 1: "The Burned Buyer"
* **Segment:** Urban Professional, high frequency grocery buyer.
* **Hypothesis 1 (Dispute Resolution):** *Confirmed.* "I bought a trimmer last month that arrived with a broken seal. When I tried to return it on the app, it got stuck in a loop asking for photos but rejecting them as 'unclear'. It took 3 days and Twitter escalation to get a refund."
