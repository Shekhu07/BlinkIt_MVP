this layout's "challenge" evidence is thin. These three findings are already established,
sourced data (present in the deck's original 3-column version of this slide) — this

   third outcome (platform switching) distinct from narrowing the basket or no change.
3. **Packaging as a distinct sub-cause** — "often packed at the bottom of the bag under
   heavier groceries" — an unprompted write-in for safer packaging, a fulfilment issue
