## Interview Guide Structure
*(Derived from Part 1 Theme: Post-Purchase Support & Refund Friction)*
- **Hypothesis 1 (Dispute Resolution):** Tell me about a time you had to request a refund or return an item on a quick-commerce app. How did the experience influence your trust in the platform?
- **Hypothesis 2 (Hidden Friction):** Have you ever decided NOT to buy a non-grocery item (like electronics or personal care) because you were worried about what would happen if it arrived damaged?
- **Hypothesis 3 (Category Expansion Trust):** What would give you the confidence to buy high-value items regularly on Blinkit?
