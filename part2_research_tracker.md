## Interview Guide Structure
*(Derived from Part 1 Themes: Trust Deficit, Handling Fees, Poor Support)*
- **Theme 1 (Trust Deficit):** Can you tell me about the last time you considered buying a non-grocery item (like personal care or electronics) on a quick-commerce app but decided against it? What held you back?
- **Theme 2 (Handling Fees):** How do you evaluate whether it's "worth it" to place an order when you only need one or two small items? 
- **Theme 3 (Poor Support):** Tell me about a time you had to return a high-value or non-grocery item. How did the experience influence your future purchasing decisions?
